What happens once the data arrive

Pages turn real by themselves

Each demo's build script reads only its input files; with real data in place, RUN.json, the figures, CLAIMS and every number on this site update automatically, and the build script removes the "internal preview" badge.

What may be said changes

Each demo's "can say / cannot say / what data would settle it" is generated from the numbers; once real data arrive, items move from the right column to the left, or stay in the middle if falsified — both outcomes are written into CLAIMS.

The stop rules are written in advance

A promoted negative control → method void; functional-prior gain < 1 % → research collaboration only; leave-species-out r < 0.8 → single-species clocks; too few rare HLA alleles → screen + sequencing confirmation. The data choose the path, not our preferences.

Three decisions for the founder (PRD §6)

  1. Whether the broiler customer's read-out product promises methylation. Birds need their own panel; the recommendation is not to promise it yet, and to start with genotypes and the ledger.
  2. How to word promise two: +50 % or +25 %. Demo 1b's decomposition says a trait without an own record reaches 50 % from r alone, while a trait recorded on the animal needs r and L together; word it per trait and fix the number after the row-1 shadow run.
  3. Where the Chinese-population HLA reference panel comes from. This decides the feasibility of the human-line wedge (row 6).
