Technology route and engines技术路线与引擎：AlphaGenome 之后的那一层。

AlphaFold solved protein structure; AlphaGenome is beginning to read the regulatory language of the genome. The next uncomputed territory is how a genome becomes traits in real lives and real environments and passes them to the next generation. We start with breeding and turn every generation into an experiment that can be computed, validated and learned from.

The layer after AlphaGenome

LayerMaturityRepresentatives
Protein sequence → structureMatureAlphaFold / ESMFold / RFdiffusion; PDB + CASP
DNA variant → molecular regulationExploding; human-centredAlphaGenome (2026-09 Atlas: 9 billion SNVs, human genome); Evo 2 (9.3 trillion nucleotides, 1 Mb context, Apache 2.0)
Genome → tissue expressionUnder construction; farm animalsFarmGTEx (Nature Genetics 2025): 16 farmed species
Genome + environment + decision → next-generation traitsEarly; Laso AI's positionGWAS / GBLUP / ssGBLUP + prospective validation; no general platform

We do not train general-purpose DNA models. Evo 2, AlphaGenome and FarmGTEx are our inputs, not our competitors. What we own is the label none of them has: genome → decision → real life → next-generation outcome.

Validation bandwidth: industry counts in millions, laboratories in thousands

Samples per year times the inverse of outcome latency. We do not race automated laboratories on speed; we compete on scale. They count in hours, we count in generations — so our moat is time and theirs is compute.
