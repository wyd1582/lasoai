Why "internal preview": the GEO pan-mammalian methylation data the PRD calls for (pig, dog, cattle, human) are not yet in hand. The engine on this page is real; the data are simulated, with every assumption listed in RUN.json. When real data arrive only the input matrix changes, and every number on this page updates itself.
For breeders: a methylation clock reads an animal's biological age, not its genotype. Its direct use in breeding is productive lifespan — sow longevity, cow productive life — turning "which animals age slowly" into a trait that can be selected and validated. Broilers are not in this demo: avian methylation differs from mammals and needs its own panel.
For AI people: the target is log relative age (age / maximum lifespan, after Lu et al. 2023); the model is an elastic net (Zou & Hastie 2005); validation has two grades: a random split (lenient) and leave-species-out (strict) — the held-out species never enters training and is predicted only through conserved sites learned from the others. Acceptance is leave-species-out r ≥ 0.8 for pig or dog, with no tuning to pass.
For the board: this is the technical shape of line D (cross-species read-out). Promise four (2035, two more healthy years for dogs and cats) and promise five (2040, biological age as a routine reading) both rest on it. This page shows the pipeline, the acceptance criterion and the report format; whether real data reach 0.8 waits for the data.

Age acceleration vs outcome: format demonstration only

Clock prediction minus the expectation at true age = age acceleration; age-adjusted, its partial correlation with the simulated pig productive lifespan is r = {{d2.accel_r}} {{d2.accel_ci}}. The estimated acceleration correlates {{d2.accel_vs_truth}} with the simulated truth. The association is planted. On real data it may be zero — in which case the read-out is positioned as "biological age", not "lifespan prediction", and promise four is reworded. That is why the report keeps "measures age" and "predicts an outcome" as two separate claims.

Probe list and thermodynamics

Ranking: absolute elastic-net coefficient → conservation pass → minimum identity. Each probe carries per-species sequence identity, GC content and SantaLucia 1998 nearest-neighbour thermodynamics (ΔH / ΔS / Tm) — a format an array-content team can pick up directly: probes_top5k.csv. In this version the sequences are random and only demonstrate the format and the calculation.

Commercial meaning

Line D · cross-species read-out

Array consumables plus read-out service. Companion-animal and longevity companies want a reproducible efficacy endpoint that is consistent across species (promise four); livestock want productive lifespan; human research cohorts want biological age as a routine reading (promise five). The deck records this as the largest addressable pool (bottom-up assumption).

Why us

Only a fixed-panel array platform can give a read-out that is consistent across species; the judge turns "is the clock accurate" into auditable numbers (leave-species-out r, intervals, an acceptance line) — the precondition for pharma and regulators to adopt it as a surrogate endpoint.

The broiler boundary

Birds need their own panel. For a white-feather broiler customer the read-out product starts with genotypes and the ledger and promises no methylation (PRD decision 1, recommended).
