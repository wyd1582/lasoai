4a · HLA imputation: by frequency stratum × panel density

{{d4.n_individuals}} simulated people ({{d4.n_train}} training, {{d4.n_test}} test), three class-I loci, one signature haplotype per allele with a mismatch rate rising from the gene centre to the window edge to mimic linkage decay. Imputation by attribute-bagged KNN; accuracy by the true allele's frequency in three strata, re-reported on panels keeping 100 % / 25 % / 8 % of MHC loci.

4b · Six-arm audit: leave-patient-out validation

{{d4.n_patients}} simulated patients × 200 candidate peptides each ({{d4.n_peptides}} in total, {{d4.n_immunogenic}} immunogenic). Metric: immunogenic peptides among each patient's top 20. Incremental gate: paired hit-rate difference against the frozen baseline, bootstrap ({{d4.n_boot}} resamples) lower bound > 0 and mean gain ≥ {{d4.min_effect}} percentage point; α = {{d4.alpha}} / attempts.

How to read arm C: the one-shot rule (affinity + expression) also passes the gate — because the planted expression signal is strong. On real data that is exactly the question an audit answers: whether a plausible-looking rule has a reproducible increment. The judge's value is that "no" is stated just as cleanly.

The audit report: written without reading the algorithm

One of the PRD's acceptance criteria is "the report can be produced without reading the algorithm's source". audit_report.en.md follows the pre-freeze independent validation format: mandate and scope → data and splits (patients, peptides, leave-patient-out, hashes) → frozen baseline and acceptance gate → results table → negative controls → conclusion (can say / cannot say) → reproduction command. Candidate algorithms appear by name only; no weights or feature recipe are in the report. In a real audit the subject party supplies a frozen prediction file (patient × peptide × score) and the auditor holds the labels.

Commercial meaning

Line E · audit and licensing

Audit fees plus pipeline milestones and revenue share; the highest assumed gross margin. Customers are personalised-therapy companies, breeding companies and pharma: what they buy is the fact of auditable validation before the freeze.

The selling point is not "more accurate"

Low absolute precision is the state of the neoantigen field (TESLA). An audit product sells reproducibility, leak-freedom and comparability: the same labels, the same gates, the same report format, for any algorithm alike.

The order of the human line

HLA typing first (read-out, the wedge), then algorithm audit (judge), then human cohorts and data licensing (memory) — matching roadmap steps two and three.
